Privacy Policy

ExcelCitizen - Canadian Citizenship Test Prep

Effective Date: April 30, 2026
Last Updated: April 30, 2026
🔒 Your Privacy Matters: ExcelCitizen is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ExcelCitizen ("the App"), developed by Curious Dev. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application designed to help newcomers and immigrants prepare for the Canadian Citizenship Test.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe and secure learning environment for all users.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

ExcelCitizen is designed to operate entirely offline and does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Browsing history or search queries
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or fitness data
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Practice Progress: Your quiz scores and progress are saved locally to track your improvement
  • Settings Preferences: Theme preferences (dark/light mode), language settings (English/French), and notification preferences
  • Achievement Data: Badges, XP, levels, and achievements earned during practice sessions
  • Study Streaks: Daily study streak counts to support your motivation
  • Bookmarked Questions: Questions you have saved for later review

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App or resetting your progress in the App's Settings screen.

4. Children's Privacy

ExcelCitizen is designed for adults preparing for the Canadian Citizenship Test. The App is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. Since we do not collect any personal information from any users, no parental consent is required.

5. Third-Party Services

ExcelCitizen does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App operates entirely independently without connecting to external servers for data collection purposes.

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store, which has its own privacy policy governing app downloads and purchases.

6. Bilingual Content

ExcelCitizen offers content in both English and French. Language preference is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server. Switching language is done entirely within the App with no external communication.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • No cloud storage or backup of your data is performed by the App

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is stored locally and visible within the App
  • Deletion: Uninstall the App to delete all associated data, or use "Reset Progress" in Settings
  • Portability: As data is stored locally, it remains on your device

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

10. App Store Requirements

This App complies with Apple's App Store Guidelines, including requirements for apps that collect no user data.
