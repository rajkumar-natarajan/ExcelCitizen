# Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 - Initial Release 🍁

*Release Date: December 24, 2025*

### 🎉 Welcome to ExcelCitizen!

ExcelCitizen is your comprehensive companion for preparing for the Canadian Citizenship Test. We're excited to launch with these features:

---

### ✨ Features

#### 📚 Comprehensive Question Bank
- **180+ practice questions** covering all citizenship test topics
- **Fully bilingual** - English and French translations for all content
- **Detailed explanations** for every answer
- Based on the official Discover Canada study guide

#### 📖 Six Topic Categories
- **Rights & Responsibilities** (28 questions)
  - Charter of Rights and Freedoms
  - Equality Rights
  - Citizenship Rights and Responsibilities
  
- **Canadian History** (34 questions)
  - Confederation and Fathers of Confederation
  - Aboriginal Peoples and First Nations
  - Exploration and Settlement
  - World Wars (WWI, WWII, Vimy Ridge, D-Day)
  - Modern Canada

- **Government & Democracy** (32 questions)
  - Federal Government structure
  - Provincial and Territorial governments
  - Municipal Government
  - Elections and Voting
  - Constitutional Monarchy

- **Geography** (30 questions)
  - All provincial and territorial capitals
  - Provinces and Territories
  - Regions of Canada
  - Natural Resources

- **Canadian Symbols** (28 questions)
  - National Symbols (flag, beaver, maple leaf)
  - National Anthem
  - Canadian Holidays
  - Flags and Emblems

- **Economy** (28 questions)
  - Trade and USMCA
  - Natural Resources
  - Major Industries
  - Agriculture
  - Banking and Currency

#### 📝 Multiple Test Modes
| Mode | Questions | Time | Best For |
|------|-----------|------|----------|
| Quick Assessment | 10 | 5 min | Daily practice |
| Standard Practice | 20 | 15 min | Regular study |
| Full Mock Exam | 20 | 30 min | Test simulation |
| Category Practice | 10 | Varies | Focused study |

#### 🏆 Gamification Features
- **XP System** - Earn experience points for every quiz
- **Level Progression** - Level up as you learn (1-50+)
- **15+ Achievements** - Unlock badges for milestones
- **Daily Streaks** - Build consistency with streak tracking
- **Progress Analytics** - Track your improvement over time

#### 🧠 Smart Learning
- **Spaced Repetition** - Questions timed for optimal retention
- **Performance Tracking** - Focus on your weak areas
- **Bookmark System** - Save questions for later review
- **Study Recommendations** - Best times to study based on your history

#### 🎨 User Experience
- **Canadian-themed Design** - Red and white aesthetic
- **Dark Mode Support** - Easy on the eyes
- **Offline Access** - No internet required
- **Universal App** - iPhone and iPad support

# Version History

## v1.0.1 (Build 2) - April 30, 2026

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Bookmark feature fixed**: The Practice screen now correctly reacts to bookmark changes made during a test session. The bookmark count and "Bookmarks" button now update immediately after returning from a quiz.
- **Bookmark practice filter fixed**: Starting a "Bookmarks" practice session now searches all available questions (not just the random subset selected for the current config), so bookmarked questions are always found correctly.

### 🧪 Tests
- Added 8 new unit tests for the bookmark feature in `SmartLearningController` covering toggle add/remove, count increment/decrement, ID set membership, immutability of returned sets, and unknown-ID lookup.
